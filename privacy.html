Privacy

What this site keeps, what it sends, and your choice about analytics.

In your browser only

Your cart, saved pieces, Maker designs and light or dark theme are kept in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device until you choose to send an order or an enquiry. They are needed for the site to work, so we don't ask for them.

Analytics, only if you agree

With your agreement we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages help, for example how many people open the Maker. It sets cookies and sends Google your pages viewed, rough location and device type. If you decline, it never loads.

When you contact us

An order or enquiry sent on WhatsApp goes through WhatsApp. One sent with the email form goes through Web3Forms to the studio's inbox. Either way we see only what you write: your name, contact details and message. We use them to reply and to fulfil your order, and keep them only as long as that takes.

Fonts and hosting

Fonts and images are served from this site. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.

Questions or deletion

Write to aayush.lilani@gmail.com to see or delete anything you've sent us.
