# The Maker · moved

The build-your-own terrarium Maker was removed from earthbox.in on 22 Oct 2026 (decision D-2026-10-22-a, owner Pravar). It now lives in the internal tool github.com/ozapravar-crypto/earthbox-studio.
`vercel.json` redirects `/maker` to the home page, so old links and shared designs don't 404; old cart lines for Maker builds are dropped silently.
