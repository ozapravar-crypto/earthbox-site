# EarthBox Care: pricing research brief (Mumbai)

*Prepared 12 Oct 2026. All sources accessed 12 Oct 2026. Prices are what each page showed that day. "Estimate" means EarthBox's own working, not a published number.*

**TL;DR:** ***A gardener visit in Mumbai costs ₹500–₹999 and a year of monthly visits costs about ₹10,000. EarthBox looks after small glass worlds, which is a specialist job, so it can charge more than a mali but should not price far above that range at launch. Suggested launch rate: ₹999 a visit (premium: ₹1,499), with 5% off 6 months and 10% off 12 months. Price kits in three bands from ₹499 to ₹1,099. Businesses start at about ₹1,499 a visit, or ₹250–₹400 per piece per month, on a 3-month minimum.***

## 3 · What one visit costs EarthBox (estimate)

**Assumptions:**
- The visit starts from Kandivali.
- The terrariums are at one address.
- The visit takes 45–60 min on site plus travel.
- Travel is by auto. The Mumbai fare from 1 Sep 2026 is ₹27 for the first 1.5 km, then ₹18.22/km ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/mumbai-auto-taxi-fare-hike-autos-to-cost-27-cabs-33-from-sept-1-commuters-fear-bigger-hit-to-household-budgets)).

| Cost | Nearby west suburbs (Borivali–Goregaon, ~8 km each way) | Far (Bandra / South Mumbai, ~25 km, or train + auto) |
|---|---|---|
| Travel, return | ≈ ₹290 (2 × ₹145) | ≈ ₹600–₹1,000 (auto) or ≈ ₹300 (train + short autos) |
| Time, door to door | ~2–2.5 h | ~3.5–4 h |
| Labour, **helper** | ₹200–₹300. Zone I minimum wage is ~₹575/day ([ksandk.com](https://ksandk.com/employment-law/calculators/minimum-wage/)), and Malad maids earn ₹10.6k–₹14k a month ([helpersnearme.com](https://helpersnearme.com/workers/mali-gardener-malad-east-mumbai)), so ~₹70–₹120/h | ₹350–₹500 |
| Labour, **Aayush himself** (time valued at ~₹500/h, an estimate) | ~₹1,000–₹1,250 | ~₹1,750–₹2,000 |
| Consumables (moss patch, soil, feed, distilled water, cloth) | ₹100–₹150 | ₹100–₹150 |
| Admin, no-shows, tool wear (+15%) | ~₹100–₹250 | ~₹150–₹450 |
| **Floor, helper visits** | **≈ ₹700–₹1,000** | **≈ ₹1,000–₹1,600** |
| **Floor, Aayush visits** | **≈ ₹1,500–₹1,900** | **≈ ₹2,300–₹3,500** |

**What this means:**
- **₹999 a visit pays its way only for nearby visits made by a helper.**
- If Aayush makes every visit himself, ₹999 loses money once his time is counted. Visits then need to be ₹1,500 or more, or grouped by area, several homes in one trip.
- Far-zone visits need a surcharge whoever makes them.

## 4 · Recommended EarthBox prices

The site's sum is *rate × visits × (1 − term discount)*. Every price below fits that sum except where marked **(needs a small site change)**.

| What | Launch (conservative) | Premium positioning | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Per-visit rate, home** (covers up to 3 EarthBox pieces, or up to 10 small plants, at one address) | **₹999** | **₹1,499** | ₹999 matches Ander Baher and sits just above Elanters and Greenlands, so it sounds familiar. ₹1,499 is a specialist price: about 1.5× a mali, and near a terrarium-workshop ticket |
| **Term discount, 6 months** | **5%** | **5%** | Benchmarks give 0–8% (Garden of Joy ~7.5%, Spaces & Wellbeing 0–3%) |
| **Term discount, 12 months** | **10%** | **8%** | A larger cut for paying up front. Premium brands discount less |
| **One-off visit / first visit** | ₹999 + **₹300 first-visit fee** | ₹1,499 + **₹500 first-visit fee** | Weak evidence. Only Elanters (visit fee, ₹199 cancellation) and Ander Baher (quote after assessment) point this way; Interioforest's site visit is free. The fee covers the first visit, which is the longest. **(needs a small site change)**: until then, write it in the order text, or waive it for plans of 6 months or more |
| **Extra pieces beyond 3 (or plants beyond 10)** | ₹150 each per visit | ₹250 each per visit | Greenlands also charges for plants above its limit **(needs a small site change)**. Otherwise use the "own plants" count to quote |
| **Far-zone surcharge** (south of Bandra, or Thane/Navi Mumbai) | ₹300 per visit | ₹500 per visit | Covers the extra travel in §3 **(quote or site change)** |
| **Custom Maker boxes** | Quote, starting at ₹1,499 a visit | Quote, starting at ₹1,999 | As the site already does |

**Worked examples (launch prices):**
- 12 months, monthly: 12 × ₹999 − 10% = **₹10,789/yr**. Greenlands charges ₹9,999 for plain plants.
- 6 months, monthly: 6 × ₹999 − 5% = **₹5,694**.
- 12 months, every 3 months: 4 × ₹999 − 10% = **₹3,596/yr**. This is the easy "set and forget" plan for closed terrariums.

**Advice on frequency:**
- Most **closed terrariums** need a visit every 3 months at most.
- Use fortnightly visits for open, carnivorous or moss pieces, and for offices.
- Default the planner to "Every month" or "Every 3 months", not "Every 2 weeks". Fortnightly visits for a closed jar look like upselling and lose trust.

## 5 · How others package their services

**Worth copying:**
- **A clear limit on what a visit covers.** Greenlands covers up to 10 plants; Elanters gives time by pot count (1–10 pots ≈ 45–60 min). EarthBox should say "up to 3 pieces / about 1 hour".
- **Prepaid plans with a refund of unused visits.** Garden of Joy refunds the unused balance and deducts ₹1,000 per visit already done. This makes a 12-month plan feel safe to buy.
- **Cancellation notice:** 24 h (Greenlands) or 3–6 h (Elanters, with a ₹199 fee if the gardener has already arrived).
- **Replacement plants, soil and manure charged extra.** Nearly everyone does this, and EarthBox's "replaced at cost" already matches.
- **A short report after each visit.** Chintaman sends monthly reports. EarthBox's "photo note after every visit" is a good selling point; keep it.
- **A short guarantee window after a visit.** Elanters gives a 3-day window; Ugaoo has a 30-day plant guarantee.
- **Seasonal advice.** Ander Baher visits more in the monsoon. For Mumbai terrariums, monsoon humidity means mould and fungus, so a "monsoon check" visit is a natural add-on.

**Pitfalls seen:**
- **Vague prices.** Ugaoo, Ander Baher and Interioforest all say "depends, get a quote", and Chintaman's own numbers contradict each other. EarthBox can stand out with a visible price.
- **Hidden extras.** Fertiliser, soil and plants are all charged extra after the visit. List exactly what the visit price includes.
- **Too many visits.** Garden plans offer 4 visits a month; terrariums don't need that, and over-servicing is a known way to kill closed terrariums.
- **Promises that are hard to keep.** "Free replacement" works for rented office pot plants (cheap, bulk) but not for a ₹10,000 chamber. Keep replacement at cost for homes.

## 6 · Questions Aayush must answer before setting prices

1. **Who makes the visits?** Aayush only, or a trained helper? This changes the floor from about ₹800 to about ₹1,800 a visit (§3).
2. **How far will you travel?** Western suburbs only (Dahisar–Andheri), or all of Mumbai? Is there a surcharge for south Mumbai, Thane or Navi Mumbai?
3. **How many pieces does one visit cover?** Up to 3 pieces, and up to 10 "own plants"?
4. **Will you visit "own plants" at all?** These are other people's plants in plastic pots, which is mali work. You could limit it to terrariums and other EarthBox-style pieces.
5. **Is there a first-visit fee, or is the first visit free with a 6- or 12-month plan?**
6. **What happens when a plant dies?** Replace at cost, or include one free replacement a year on 12-month plans?
7. **What's in each kit, exactly?** Which brand of mix and feed; how many grams. The kit cost decides the band.
8. **Prepaid plans?** Refund unused visits if they cancel?
9. **Businesses:** what is the minimum contract, and do you want offices at all in year one, given they take more time per visit?
10. **GST and invoices:** registered or not? Businesses usually want a GST invoice.

## Evidence gaps (honest list)

- **No Indian terrarium-specific maintenance price was found anywhere.** All visit benchmarks are gardener/mali services, so the specialist premium is a judgement call.
- Ugaoo, Nurserylive and Urban Company do not publish garden-service prices online. The Urban Company app may show a Mumbai gardener rate after you set a location; worth checking by phone.
- Indian prices for carnivorous mix, air-plant feed and orchid bark were not found, so the kit costs for those are estimates.
- The aquarium-maintenance figures and some Ugaoo kit prices come from search summaries; their pages were not opened.
- The minimum-wage figure differs between sources (₹535–₹575/day, Zone I).

**Cheapest way to close these gaps:** call 2–3 Mumbai services (Ugaoo, Ander Baher, Greenlands) as a customer, and ask the price of one visit for 3 terrariums in Kandivali.
